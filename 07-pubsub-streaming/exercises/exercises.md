# Module 07 — Exercises

1. Create a topic + pull subscription with 60s ack deadline and exactly-once enabled.
2. Publish 1,000 messages with attributes and an ordering key.
3. Difference between pull and push subscriptions — give a use case for each.
4. Configure a dead-letter topic with max 3 delivery attempts.
5. Why is "push subscription to Dataflow" wrong? What is the correct Dataflow pattern?
6. What do snapshots and seek let you do?
7. What are ordering keys? Do they guarantee global ordering?
8. What does the schema registry enforce and at which stage?
9. What happens if a subscriber nacks or times out on ack?
10. How do you achieve effectively-once end-to-end (Pub/Sub + downstream)?
